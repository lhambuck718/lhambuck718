Welcome to my GitHub profile!

I'm a PhD student in evolutionary genomics at the University of Liège (Belgium).

My research focuses on understanding photosynthesis in early-diverging cyanobacteria, investigating how thylakoid membranes emerged and evolved, and exploring how their evolution may have been shaped by environmental conditions during the Proterozoic.

What I particularly enjoy about this project is trying to piece together the evolutionary history of cyanobacteria by looking for clues in their genomes. By comparing gene content and organization, exploring protein sequences, structures, and reconstructing phylogenetic relationships across different organisms, I try to understand how these biological systems evolved. What I find most exciting is bringing together all these different perspectives to better understand how thylakoid membranes emerged.

If you're curious about what I'm working on: 
https://www.sciences.uliege.be/cms/c_13690850/en/how-cyanobacteria-developed-photosynthetic-membranes-over-the-course-of-evolution

Beyond my research, I'm also interested in making science more open and accessible. I try to contribute to this by using open-source tools whenever possible, sharing code and data, and documenting my analyses so that others can understand and reuse them. I also enjoy exploring different ways of communicating and sharing scientific knowledge.

This GitHub is part of that effort, and I hope to gradually make more of my research resources available here!
